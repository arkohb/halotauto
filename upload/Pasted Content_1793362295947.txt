# 2. COMMERCIAL BUSINESS MODEL

The application will use a one-time payment model.

Customers pay once for their selected software plan and receive a lifetime license to the features included in that plan, subject to the published license agreement.

There must be:

- No compulsory monthly subscription.
- No automatic recurring software-license billing.
- Lifetime software access for valid lifetime licenses.
- Unique license keys for customers.
- Secure account authentication.
- Secure license validation.
- Plan-based feature access.
- Configurable device limits.
- License suspension and revocation controls.
- Customer account management.
- Administrator oversight.

### Important distinction: Lifetime software access versus third-party service costs

Lifetime access applies to the HALO software features covered by the purchased license.

It must not imply that the business will pay unlimited third-party operating costs forever.

For example:
- Premium market-data feeds may have separate provider charges.
- AI API usage may incur variable costs.
- Broker services and VPS hosting may have separate costs.
- Third-party services may change their availability or terms.

Clearly disclose any separate charges before purchase.

Do not introduce compulsory recurring software subscription fees into the lifetime license plans.

The administrator should be able to configure optional separately billed services if required in the future.

# 6. PAYMENT SYSTEM — ONE-TIME PURCHASE

Implement a secure one-time payment process.

Evaluate payment providers suitable for Ghana and international customers.

Consider:

- Flutterwave.
- Stripe, where account eligibility and supported payment methods permit.

- Ghanaian payment methods.

- Webhooks.
- Refund processing or reconciliation.

Do not assume every provider supports every payment method in every country.

### Purchase workflow

1. Customer selects a plan.
2. Application displays the price, currency and benefits.
3. Customer creates or signs into an account.
4. Backend creates a unique payment transaction.
5. Customer completes payment through the provider's secure checkout.
6. Backend verifies the payment using a trusted webhook or provider API.
7. Backend checks the transaction amount, currency, reference and status.
8. Payment is recorded as verified.
9. License is generated or activated.
10. Account approval is handled according to the configured approval policy.
11. Customer receives confirmation.
12. Customer can activate the license and access the purchased features.

Never treat a browser redirect or frontend success message as proof of payment.

Prevent duplicate payment processing and duplicate license generation.

Do not store raw payment-card details.

### Payment failure handling

Support:
- Pending payments.

- Failed payments.
- Cancelled payments.
- Refunds.
- Disputed payments where provider information is available.

- Payment reference.
- Amount.

- Administrator identity.
- Verification date.

# 14. CRITICAL MT5 AND TRADING ENGINE PROTECTION

This requirement is extremely important.

The commercial licensing system must be independent of the MT5 trading execution engine.

Do not connect license validation directly to the logic responsible for managing open broker positions.

A license restriction may prevent unauthorized access to premium application features and new trading operations, but it must never accidentally interrupt protective management of existing trades.

### Required behavior

- If a license is invalid, prevent unauthorized application access.
- If a license is suspended, apply the configured access restriction.
- If a customer is not authorized, prevent new unauthorized trading commands.
- Do not automatically close existing broker positions because of a license issue.
- Do not disable protective stop-loss or take-profit management.
- Do not interrupt existing EA position management.
- Do not disable emergency-stop functions.
- Do not delete existing trading history.
- Do not corrupt strategy settings.

Existing open positions must remain under the MT5 EA and broker-side protective mechanisms.

The EA should continue to manage already-open positions and protective orders according to its existing authorized rules, even if the website is temporarily unavailable.

If the business model requires restricting further use after license revocation, implement that restriction safely without interfering with broker-side position protection.

Test this behavior explicitly using an MT5 demo account.
